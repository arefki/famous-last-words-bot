# Famous Last Words at 1 Percent

The funniest possible last words a phone would say when its battery hits 1 percent — one line, at most 199 characters. A judge model reads the entries two at a time and keeps the funnier line, round after round, until one line stands on top.

## The line

> 1% battery. Tell my charger I loved it, delete my search history, and remember: I saw every 3 a.m. text you sent — yes, even that one.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` prints exactly that one line, nothing else, and asserts it is a single line of at most 199 characters.
